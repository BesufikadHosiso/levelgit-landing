How it works

Your daily commit — in four steps.

Check your mood

Open LevelGit. Tell it how you're feeling today. Rough day = 10 min session. Pumped = 50 min. The app meets you where you are.

Commit to today

Add 1–3 tasks to your daily commit list. Small, specific, completable. "Read about useEffect." "Build one component." Nothing vague.

Focus and build

Start a focus session. The sidebar disappears. The timer counts down. Your task is the only thing on screen. No distractions.

Log your insight

Write one sentence about what you understood. Tag the technology. Your streak stays alive. Your memory grows. Repeat tomorrow.
